description=[/list]

description=[h2]🚀 Quick Start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Get a [b]Vehicle Tag[/b] and a [b]Gate Reader[/b]: craft them, or find them at mechanics and gas stations
description=[*] Open the vehicle mechanics panel and install the tag under "Knox Pass"
description=[*] With the reader and a screwdriver, right-click a gate and choose [b]Install Knox Pass reader[/b]
description=[*] Open [b]Manage reader[/b] from the right-click menu and register the cars parked nearby
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Hands-free gates[/b]: drive up from the driver's seat and the gate opens, then closes after you leave
description=[*] [b]Build your own gates[/b]: boom barriers, roll-up garage doors and two-story gates, wide enough for several cars side by side
description=[*] [b]Reads on the move[/b]: the faster you drive, the earlier it opens; self-driving cars find it already open
description=[*] [b]A real vehicle part[/b]: fits vanilla and modded cars, and moves with the tag to another car
description=[*] [b]Per-gate registration[/b]: only the cars you register get through, and each gate has its own range, close delay, speed and lock
description=[*] [b]Works with many gates[/b]: fence gates, double doors, garage doors and player-built doors
description=[*] [b]7 shell colors[/b]: craft tags and readers in color with vanilla paint, or repaint the ones you have
description=[*] [b]No crashes, no pinching[/b]: warns you ahead of a gate that won't open, and never closes on a car or a person
description=[/list]
description=📖 [b]Installing readers, registering cars, building gates, per-gate settings, shell colors, getting the items and FAQ:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/3814684002/586187800874015404/]Knox Pass Guide: Tags, Readers & Hands-free Gates[/url]
