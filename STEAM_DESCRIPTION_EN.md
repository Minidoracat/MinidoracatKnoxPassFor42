[h1]📡 Minidoracat Knox Pass for B42[/h1]
[h3]By Minidoracat[/h3]

[hr][/hr]

Put a tag in your car and a reader on your gate: drive up and the gate opens by itself, then closes behind you.

[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] Requires the UI framework: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789836701]Minidoracat UI Library for B42[/url]
[/list]

[h2]🚀 Quick Start[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Get a [b]Vehicle Tag[/b] and a [b]Gate Reader[/b]: craft them, or find them at mechanics and gas stations
[*] Open the vehicle mechanics panel and install the tag under "Knox Pass"
[*] With the reader and a screwdriver, right-click a gate and choose [b]Install Knox Pass reader[/b]
[*] Open [b]Manage reader[/b] from the right-click menu and register the cars parked nearby
[/olist]

[h2]✨ Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Hands-free gates[/b]: drive up from the driver's seat and the gate opens, then closes after you leave
[*] [b]Boom barriers[/b]: build your own lift-arm barrier; red means stop, green means go
[*] [b]Reads on the move[/b]: the faster you drive, the earlier it opens; self-driving cars find it already open
[*] [b]A real vehicle part[/b]: fits vanilla and modded cars, and moves with the tag to another car
[*] [b]Per-gate registration[/b]: only the cars you register get through, and you can lock it to Knox Pass only
[*] [b]Works with many gates[/b]: fence gates, double doors, garage doors and player-built doors
[*] [b]7 shell colors[/b]: repaint tags and readers with vanilla paint and a paintbrush; registrations stay intact
[*] [b]No crashes, no pinching[/b]: warns you ahead of a gate that won't open, and never closes on a car or a person
[*] [b]Server settings[/b]: read range, close delay, power and tag battery are all adjustable
[/list]
